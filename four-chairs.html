Four Chairs

Lorenzo Marco Besana

Observation · Week 1 3 in attendance

EXT. BUILDING - DAY

It's cold.

There are four4 chairs and three people in front of a door.

The man [RE: Man] and the younger man [RE: Student] sit in front of each other. The woman [RE: Supervisor] stands behind the Man.

The Supervisor and the Man wear a winter jacket, while the Student is in a T-shirt.

The Man and the Student smoke with their head to the ground. The Supervisor is observing the Student.

He notices her stare.

Student

What?

Supervisor

Where the fuck is your jacket?

The Man snaps back.

Man

HEY! What's with the language.

Student

I don't have a jacket.

Supervisor

It's December why is he in a fucking T-shirt?

The Man stands up and turns to her.

Man

You really can't talk to him like that.

Student

I can speak up for myself, you know?

Man

Shut up and go finish setting up.

The Student throws his cigarette to the ground and heads inside.

Student

This some bullshit.

Supervisor

Hey!

Man

Let him go.

The Student enters the building.

Man

What the fuck was that?

Supervisor

He should have his jacket.

Man

Well he didn't bring, so what now?

She scoffs and heads inside. He follows her.

INT. ROOM - CONTINUOUS

There is a BLACK HOLE LEVITATING IN THE MIDDLE OF THE ROOM. It doesn't emit any sound, it doesn't move.

On the right a large table is crowded with all sorts of science equipment.

The Student is kneeled at a safe distance across from it. He is setting up McGuffin #1 behind an orange tape line.

Man

Is it ready?

The Student turns to them, tilts his head.

Student

Yeah?

Supervisor

Are you confused about it?

Student

I didn't set it up.

Man

So it's not ready.

Student

No. It's ready. It's already been calibrated.

Supervisor

Do you even know how?

Student

I do not.

Supervisor

(whispered)

Just go check it out.

The Man approaches the Student.

We stay on the Supervisor. Her attention diverts to the table. Something doesn't add up.

CLOSE ON STUDENT AND MAN

The man kneels next to the Student to observe McGuffin #1.

Man

Let's see.

The man starts doing [INSERT SCIENCE THINGY]. We notice his right hand is MISSING the PINKY.

Student

By the way I never asked you what happened there.

Man

Where?

The Student nods to Man's hand. The Man doesn't understand.

Student

Your pinky. How'd you lose it?

The Man looks down at his own hand.

Man

What— It's just the way it is.

Student

Does it ever hurt?

The man closes his fist, closely observing his four4 fingers.

Supervisor (O.C.)

What the fuck is this?

The man and the Student turn to the Supervisor. She is holding a TAPE RECORDER.

The man and the Student stand up.

Man

What about it?

Supervisor

Did you play it?

Student

Why would we play it? We the ones doing the recording.

Supervisor

You are fucking with it, aren't you? You are fucking with me. You always are.

Man

Nora. You need to calm down.

Supervisor

I need to— Sure. Listen to this. This little shit is fucking with me.

She plays the tape. [Text in italic is on tape].

Man

First week of observation failed to produce any conclusive results.

Student

First week?

Man

The anomaly doesn't appear to weigh anything as well as it doesn't show any signs of emitting nor absorbing or attracting anything.

The tape keeps playing with no voice on it.

Man

Exactly.

The tape keeps playing. No voice. On the tape we hear the Supervisor and the Man laughing thunderously.

Supervisor

(catching her breath)

Alright. Alright. All jokes aside. This anomaly might be a unicum in human history. We may have never seen anything like this before.

The Supervisor stops the recording.

Man

How did you do this?

Student

Do what?

Supervisor

You understand you are fucking done, right?

She starts going through the table.

Supervisor (CONT'D)

Four4 cups, four4 pairs of gloves.

She points in a corner.

Supervisor (CONT'D)

Those random shoes. What else do you have in your backpack? A fourth4 toothbrush? It doesn't even make sense.

She lifts her right hand. She is wearing a wedding ring.

Supervisor

Did you put this on me in my sleep? That's sick. You couldn't even bother to put any initials on it? Just mine? That's not how wedding rings work. You are supposed to be a PhD student, dumbass.

Student

What in the actual fuck are you talking about?

As the Supervisor and the Man keep arguing the man looks back down at his four fingers.

Man

Nora, how many fingers do I have?

Supervisor

What?

Man

On my right hand. How many fingers?

Supervisor

What kind of question is that?

Man

We've known each other 20 years. It's a simple question.

He raises his hands. Showing the four fingers.

She steps back.

Man

There are four3 chairs outside, Nora.

Supervisor

It's not possible.

Man

The McGuffin #1 was calibrated already.

The three of them look at each other and distance themselves from one another. They turn to the black hole.

The McGuffin #1 flares up.

Nora drops to her knees.

She starts screaming her lungs out.

CAMERA PANS OUT.

THE END.

It doesn't emit any sound.
It doesn't move.
